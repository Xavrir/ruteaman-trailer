# RuteAman trailer: music guide

The video has no audio track. These notes are for adding a score.

## Tempo

**Recommended: 72 BPM, cinematic restraint (60 to 80 BPM range).** The cut timing is built on this grid: one beat = 0.833 s = 25 frames at 30 fps, one bar of 4/4 = 3.333 s. Every scene cut lands on a beat. If you choose another tempo in the 60 to 80 range, align the track's downbeat with 0:00 and check the hard cuts at 41.67 s and 45.00 s.

The alternative is 90 to 110 BPM (effortless flow). Use it only if the track has a half-time feel that still lands on the cuts below. Its energy fights the night mood.

## Track search terms

Search YouTube Audio Library or Pixabay Music for:
- "cinematic ambient piano 72 bpm", "minimal emotional piano night"
- "soft hopeful underscore", "calm corporate ambient" (avoid upbeat corporate)
- "night city ambient pad", "lofi ambient slow"
- Filters: mood calm or inspirational, no vocals, 60 to 80 BPM, at least 55 s long, or with a loopable section.

Avoid tracks with strong drops, vocals, or a busy hi-hat: they compete with the on-screen text.
